Logistics and cargo

Move items from one place to another

RushCargo is the logistics arm of RUSH. It delivers every order from RUSH stores, restaurants and pharmacies, and you can book it to move any item you need, from a motorcycle for a small parcel to a large van for bulk stock. Follow it until it is delivered.

Store orders, including heavy RushEssentials goods such as appliances and construction materials, ride on RushCargo in a vehicle matched to their weight and size.

Book cargo in the RUSH app. Drivers use the RushCargo app.

RushCargo moves items only. People, animals, weapons and illegal or dangerous goods are never carried. Bookings that break this rule are refused. For passenger rides, use RushTranspo.

Vehicles

Choose the vehicle for your load

Each vehicle lists its weight limit, cargo space and examples. RUSH also suggests the smallest vehicle that fits the weight and size of your items.

How booking works

  1. Set the pickup and drop-off

    Enter both addresses. The distance and fare are calculated before you book.

  2. Describe the item

    Give the category, a description, the weight and the number of pieces, so the right vehicle is offered.

  3. Pay and get a driver

    Pay through RushPay. Once the payment is confirmed, the job goes to nearby drivers whose vehicle can carry the load.

  4. Hand over and track

    Follow the driver on the map, and give the handover PIN when the item arrives.

Become a RushCargo driver

Drivers apply in the RushCargo app with their licence, vehicle and documents. Once approved, you go online when you are ready to work and see the jobs your vehicle can carry near you. You can filter jobs by distance, and your earnings are paid out to the bank, GCash or Maya account you add.
