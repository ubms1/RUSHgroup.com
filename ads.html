RushAds · Advertising

Reach RUSH customers where they shop, eat and book care

RushAds is the advertising programme for merchants, health providers and brands on RUSH. Promote your store, product, service or offer to the people who use the RUSH app for groceries, food, medicines, deliveries and health care.

Advertisers reach RUSH customers through the RUSH app and the RUSH website.

What RushAds covers

Three ways to promote on RUSH

Store and product placements

Feature your grocery store, restaurant, pharmacy or products to customers browsing near them.

Promotions and vouchers

Link a campaign to a RUSH voucher that customers can apply at checkout, so the offer is easy to use.

Measure the results

Follow the orders and sales that come from your campaign in the merchant analytics your store already uses.

Who advertises with RushAds

  • Grocery stores, restaurants and pharmacies on RUSH.
  • Doctors, clinics, laboratories and hospitals on RushMedz.
  • Brands that sell products to RUSH customers.

Advertising standards

Every RushAds campaign follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and RUSH policies. Campaigns may not promote anything that is illegal, misleading or that breaks the RushCargo rule against carrying people or animals. Health claims must be accurate and must comply with the rules for medicines and health services.
