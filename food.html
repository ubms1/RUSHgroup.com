Restaurants and food

Restaurant food, delivered to you

Order from restaurants and food providers near you. Each restaurant sets its preparation time and minimum order, and you see both before you pay. A RushCargo rider delivers your meal.

Order food in the RUSH app, on Android and iPhone.

How ordering works

  • Find restaurants by name, cuisine and distance.
  • See each restaurant's opening status, preparation time and minimum order.
  • Add dishes with notes, such as no onions, and check the total with delivery.
  • Pay through RushPay at checkout. The restaurant gets your order once the payment is confirmed, and you follow it to your door.

For restaurants

RushFood is the restaurant app. Restaurant owners use it to:

  • Receive new orders as they come in, and accept, prepare, mark ready or reject them.
  • Set the menu, prices, sale prices and availability for each dish.
  • Set preparation time, minimum order, delivery radius and opening hours.
  • Follow sales, deliveries and reviews. Sales are paid out to the bank, GCash or Maya account the restaurant adds.

Delivery

Riders from RushCargo

Each restaurant order is booked to RushCargo as soon as the restaurant accepts it. Nearby drivers who can carry the order are offered the job, and the rider collects it from the restaurant and brings it to you. You get a handover PIN for the drop-off.
