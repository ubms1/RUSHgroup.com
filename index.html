What RUSH offers

Industries we serve

RUSH brings these industries together on one account, one payment service and one delivery network. Customers use the RUSH app and RushPay; businesses and professionals use the partner app for their trade.

How it works

From account to delivery in three steps

  1. Create your account

    Register with your mobile number or email. A one-time code confirms it is you. One account works everywhere on RUSH.

  2. Choose a service

    Pick a store, a restaurant, a pharmacy, a ride, an essentials shop, a cargo booking or a health service. Prices, fares and delivery fees are shown before you pay.

  3. Pay and follow it live

    Pay through RushPay at checkout. Your order goes to the store once the payment is confirmed, and you can follow it until a RushCargo driver brings it to you.

Every delivery goes to RushCargo. Orders from RUSH stores, restaurants, pharmacies and essentials shops, and cargo bookings, are sent automatically to nearby RushCargo drivers. Each order is matched to a vehicle that fits its weight and size. Rides go to verified RushTranspo drivers the same way.
