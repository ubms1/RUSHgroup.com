Groceries and retail

Groceries from stores near you

Shop from grocery stores, mini-marts and specialty shops that deliver to you. See what is in stock, add what you need, apply a promo code and pay through RushPay. A RushCargo rider brings your order to your door.

Shopping happens in the RUSH app, on Android and iPhone.

How shopping works

  • Stores show their categories, whether they are open, and the delivery radius they serve.
  • Products show their price, any sale price, and whether they are in stock.
  • Your delivery fee is worked out from the distance to your address before you pay.
  • Apply a promo code at checkout. The discount is shown before you confirm.
  • Pay through RushPay at checkout. The store gets your order once the payment is confirmed, and you follow each step of the delivery live.

For grocery stores

RushGrocery is the store app. A store owner can:

  • Accept orders, start preparing them, mark them ready and reject an order with a reason.
  • Manage products, prices, sale prices and stock, with a record of every stock change.
  • Give each product a weight and size, so RUSH can choose the right RushCargo vehicle.
  • Create store vouchers and follow sales, orders and reviews. Sales are paid out to the bank, GCash or Maya account the store adds.

Delivery

Delivered by RushCargo

When a store accepts your order, RUSH books the delivery to RushCargo automatically. The order goes to nearby drivers whose vehicle can carry its weight and size, from motorcycles for small and medium orders to tricycles, sedans and vans for heavier loads.
